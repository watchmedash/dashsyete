# Dash City — Map v2 (Islands) + Gameplay Fixes

**Date:** 2026-07-13
**Status:** Approved for planning
**Supersedes:** the city-layout section of `2026-07-12-dash-city-design.md`; all other v1 rules stay in force.

## Overview

Replace the single-landmass city with a bigger 5-island archipelago: one themed island per team plus a central free-for-all island, connected by bridges over water. Bundle in fixes observed in playtesting: broken road connectivity, drive-through buildings, name-label style, cars flipping/stuck-on-side/sinking, and HP bar sizing.

## Island world

- Grid grows from 24×24 to **48×48 tiles** (576 m × 576 m world). `TILE` stays 12 m.
- **Water** covers the whole map at y ≈ −2 (visual plane; no collider). Islands are elevated ground slabs with tops at y = 0.
- The city map data (`shared/src/cityMap.ts`) gains a `grounds` list (world-space rectangles); the server builds one fixed ground cuboid per rectangle (replacing the map-wide slab), the client builds one ground mesh per rectangle plus the water plane. No arena walls — the sea is the boundary.

### Islands

| Island | Position | Size | Theme | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Center | middle | ~16×16 tiles | Downtown: commercial skyscrapers, ring road, roundabout | Free-for-all; no spawns |
| Crimson | North | ~12×12 | Low-rise commercial uptown (`building-a..h`, awnings) | Distinct from center high-rises |
| Azure | East | ~12×12 | Industrial harbor: warehouses, chimneys, tanks, docked boats offshore | |
| Emerald | South | ~12×12 | Suburbs: houses, trees, fences | |
| Violet | West | ~12×12 | Graveyard old town: crypts, gravestones, pines, iron fences | |

Each team island has its spawn plaza (6+ slots) and a small internal road grid connected to its bridges.

### Island dressing — use the packs fully

Every island gets a distinct, densely dressed identity (visual-only props need no colliders; chunky ones get measured colliders):

- **Center / Downtown:** skyscraper canyon around the roundabout, street lights along avenues, a **construction zone** (barriers, cones, construction lights) and **4 ramp/jump spots** built from `road-slant`/`tile-high` pieces for launching cars, parasol-covered plaza corners.
- **Crimson / Uptown:** low-rise shops with awnings + overhangs, parasols, planters, street lights, cone-scattered side streets.
- **Azure / Harbor:** warehouses, chimneys, tanks, a **freight yard** of parked train carriages (static obstacles — the train kit's new home), stacked cargo containers and cargo piles on the docks, tug/speed/fishing boats moored offshore, buoys marking the bridge channels.
- **Emerald / Suburbs:** houses with driveways and stone paths, fenced yards, tree clusters, planters, hay bales.
- **Violet / Old Town:** crypt lanes, gravestone fields, pines, iron-fence borders with gates, lightposts, altar + coffin corners, pumpkin patches, and the **ghost ship** (`ship-small-ghost`) anchored just offshore.

**Dynamic props (new):** ~24 small physics props scattered across the islands — cones, boxes, hay bales, pumpkins — simulated on the server as light dynamic bodies, broadcast in snapshots as `prop-<n>` entries, knockable but harmless. They make ramming feel physical.

**The sea lives too:** a **cargo ship** (kinematic, like the old train) sails a slow loop around the center island, passing under no bridges (route stays in open water between the spokes); decorative sail ships and buoys anchor the corners of the map. The old perimeter *train* is retired in favor of the harbor freight yard and the sailing ship.

### Bridges (8 total)

- **4 spokes:** one straight `road-bridge` run from each team island to the center island.
- **4 ring links:** neighboring team islands connect via an L-shaped route that bends on a small **corner islet** (a few ground tiles with a road bend) — grid bridges cannot run diagonally.
- Bridge decks are at road level (y = 0) with their own ground collider strip.
- Bridges get **low side-rail colliders** (and the model's visual railings) so cars don't casually slide off; island shorelines stay open — shoving enemies into the sea is allowed and fun.

### Water hazard

- Driving off a shoreline drops the car into the water. When a car's y falls below the kill threshold (just under water level), it force-respawns at its team plaza — **no attribution, no score change** (same rule as flip respawns). No splash effect in this version.

### Bot routing

- Each bot's cruise route: home-island loop → spoke bridge → center ring road → back. Routes are explicit ordered waypoint lists in the map data (all points on roads/bridges). Hunting behavior unchanged.

## Fixes

1. **Road connectivity:** the v1 junction/bend rotations were guessed and are visibly wrong. Every road piece rotation used by the generator is verified at street level (screenshots) before the map is considered done.
2. **Measured colliders:** a `MODEL_FOOTPRINTS` table (checked into `shared/`, generated by measuring GLB bounding boxes — center offset and half-extents at native scale) replaces tile-sized guesses. Building colliders come from measured footprint × pack scale. This fixes both drive-through walls and invisible oversized walls.
3. **Name labels:** plain text sprite — team-colored fill with a dark outline, no background pill. The team underglow ring under cars is removed.
4. **Anti-flip physics:**
   - Lower chassis center of mass (Rapier mass-properties offset).
   - Speed-sensitive steering: full lock at low speed, reduced lock as speed rises (primary flip cause).
   - Flip detection triggers when the car's up-vector y < 0.3 (catches resting-on-side, not just fully inverted) for the usual `FLIP_RESPAWN_S`.
5. **Car visual anchoring:** car models are offset inside their root so the wheels sit on the road (currently the model clips into the ground / "sinks"). Verified visually.
6. **HP bar:** 18 px → 8 px tall, same placement and color behavior.

## Testing

- Extend `cityMap` integrity tests: 4 team spawns on their island's ground rect; every waypoint on a road/bridge tile; every bridge run connects two ground rects; dynamic prop spawn points on ground; determinism preserved.
- Sim test: a car tipped onto its side is detected by flip detection.
- Sim test: a car placed off-shore falls below the kill threshold (ground doesn't extend past islands).
- Manual: street-level screenshot review of each junction type; two-tab playtest; `scripts/watch-bots.mjs` still shows a healthy arena (movement, damage, knockouts) with the new routes.

## Out of scope

- Water buoyancy / splash particles (the car simply sinks, then respawns)
- Boats as drivable vehicles
- Minimap (bigger world may warrant one later)
- Reconnect grace period (still deferred from v1)
